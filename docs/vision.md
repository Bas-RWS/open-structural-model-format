# OSMF Vision

## 1. Purpose

The Open Structural Model Format (OSMF) aims to provide an open, vendor-neutral and durable format for structural engineering information.

Structural information is currently distributed across modeling software, analysis software, inspection systems, monitoring platforms, measurement tools and document repositories. This makes information difficult to exchange, combine, reuse and preserve.

OSMF aims to provide a common framework for describing, connecting and preserving this information.

## 2. Scope

OSMF is intended to cover the lifecycle of structures and infrastructure, including:

- Conceptual and geometric modeling
- Structural idealization
- Finite element analysis models
- Computational results
- Inspections and condition assessments
- Reality capture
- Measurements and structural health monitoring
- Engineering libraries and reference data
- Historical information and provenance

The format is intended for bridges, buildings, tunnels and other structures.

## 3. Vision

Engineering data should remain accessible and meaningful regardless of the software used to create, modify, analyze or inspect it.

OSMF aims to enable data to be:

- Exchanged between software applications
- Reused for different purposes
- Connected across engineering domains
- Preserved over long periods
- Interpreted by both humans and machines
- Used in automated and AI-assisted workflows

## 4. Independence

OSMF does not prescribe a particular software application, solver, modeling method or engineering workflow.

Domains may exist independently and may be connected when relevant.

## 5. Long-term ambition

OSMF aims to become a reliable common data foundation for structural engineering, supporting open collaboration, innovation, interoperability and long-term preservation.
